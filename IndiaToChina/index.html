India Market & Policy Intelligence
MARKET
India's construction equipment industry crosses 1.4 lakh units in FY26
India's construction-equipment industry recorded 1,40,191 units in total sales during FY26. Exports increased by 31.5% year-on-year to 16,885 units, reflecting strong infrastructure demand, mechanisation and growing manufacturing capability.
Source: IBEF / Ministry of Heavy Industries
INFRASTRUCTURE POLICY
PM GatiShakti is expanding India's infrastructure pipeline
As of August 2026, 396 infrastructure projects worth approximately ₹18.66 lakh crore had been evaluated through the Network Planning Group. Of these, 256 projects had been sanctioned and 198 were under implementation. The framework covers roads, railways, urban infrastructure, ports, industrial development and other connected sectors.
Source: Press Information Bureau, Government of India
GOVERNMENT INVESTMENT
FY27 capital expenditure reaches ₹12.22 lakh crore
The Union Budget 2026–27 provides ₹12.22 lakh crore for capital expenditure, equivalent to approximately 3.1% of GDP. Continued public investment creates opportunities across transportation, infrastructure, industrial development and construction activity.
Source: Union Budget 2026–27
EQUIPMENT POLICY
New Construction & Infrastructure Equipment scheme focuses on domestic capability
The Union Budget 2026–27 announced a new Construction & Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) scheme with a total financial outlay of ₹14,300 crore over seven years. The policy direction is aimed at strengthening domestic capability, encouraging investment, supporting value addition and reducing import dependence.
Source: Press Information Bureau / Government of India
MANUFACTURING
India's manufacturing ecosystem continues to expand
Government PLI programmes across 14 sectors have attracted more than ₹2.40 lakh crore of actual investment as of March 2026 and generated more than 14.15 lakh direct and indirect jobs. This broader manufacturing push supports localisation, industrial investment and technology adoption.
Source: Press Information Bureau / Government of India
